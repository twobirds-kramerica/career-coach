Why it earns a place in your search

Every analysis ends in one clear recommendation with its reasoning. No dashboards with twenty metrics. One posting, three scores, one call.

02

The working loop

One job posting at a time, verified before you build on it. Under thirty minutes from posting to submitted application.

Verify the posting

Gate Zero checks the link first. Aggregator reposts and dead listings get flagged before you build anything on them.

Get the verdict

ATS match, overall fit, and salary signal, scored against your CV and your stated priorities. Then one clear recommendation.

Build the package

Tailored CV suggestions and a cover letter draft that use your real experience. You edit, you approve, you send.

Keep the momentum

Every application lands in your pipeline with a follow-up date. The tracker surfaces what needs action next, not just a status label.
