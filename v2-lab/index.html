05

What actually moves an application forward

The advice below is what Career Coach's own tools are built around. None of it requires the app; the app just makes it fast.

Use the posting's exact words, honestly

Screening software matches literally. If the posting says "project management" and you have it, those exact words belong on your CV. Never add words for skills you do not have; the interview will find you out.

Keep the CV parseable

Standard headings, a single column, no tables or text boxes in the body. The most beautiful layout loses to the software that cannot read it.

Quality beats volume, measurably

Canadian hiring managers are drowning in near-identical AI-generated applications; that is the pile a generic submission joins. One application tailored to a verified posting outworks five sprayed ones.

Apply at the source

Aggregator reposts go stale and sometimes never reach the employer. Find the posting on the employer's own careers site before you invest anything.

Let AI research, not impersonate

Four in five Canadian hiring managers say they can spot a wholly AI-written application. Use the AI for the analysis and the first draft; the final words should sound like you, because they are yours.

Set a follow-up date the moment you apply

Don't leave the follow-up to memory. A short, specific note a week later beats a perfect one you never send — the tracker holds the date and surfaces it when it's due.
