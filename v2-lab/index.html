01

The working loop

One job posting at a time, verified before you spend a minute on it. Under 30 minutes from posting to submitted application.

Verify the posting

Gate Zero checks the link first. Aggregator reposts and dead listings get flagged before you build anything on them.

Get the verdict

ATS match, overall fit, and salary signal, scored against your CV and your stated priorities. Then one clear recommendation.

Build the package

Tailored CV tweaks and a cover letter draft that use your real experience. Nothing invented, everything defensible in an interview.

Keep the momentum

Every application lands in your pipeline with a follow-up date. The tool shows what needs action next, not just a status label.

02

What a verdict looks like

No dashboards with twenty metrics. One posting, three scores, one recommendation, and the reason behind it.

Sample analysis

Senior Product Manager · Acme Health Tech · Toronto, hybrid

Verdict: apply custom ATS match 82 Overall fit 74 Salary: at target

Why: your platform and stakeholder experience covers eight of the ten stated requirements. The gap is healthcare domain language. A custom CV that surfaces your regulated-industry work closes most of it. Worth the thirty minutes.

03

Seven tools, one job at a time

Everything orbits the posting in front of you. No tool asks you to start from a blank page.

The anchor

Posting analysis

Scores the match, flags the gaps, names the strengths, and gives the apply-or-skip verdict with its reasoning.

Gate zero

Link verification

Detects aggregator reposts and unreachable postings so you never build a package on a dead listing.

Keywords

ATS analysis

Maps the posting's screening keywords against your CV before you submit, so the robot reads what the human meant.

First draft

Cover letter

Drafts from your real experience and the posting's actual asks. You edit, you approve, you send.

Preparation

Interview prep

Likely questions from this specific posting, with answer scaffolds built on your own track record.

Numbers

Salary negotiation

A range for the role and region, plus what to say and what not to say when the offer conversation starts.

The long game

Pipeline tracker

Applications, statuses, and follow-up dates in one place, with overdue items surfaced as actions, not archives.

04

Your key, your data, your call

Bring your own AI

Career Coach connects to the AI provider you choose: Claude, GPT, Gemini, or a local Ollama model that never leaves your machine. Your API key and your CV are stored in your browser only.

That is a deliberate design choice, not a limitation. Nobody mines your job search. There is no account, no server, and nothing to leak.
