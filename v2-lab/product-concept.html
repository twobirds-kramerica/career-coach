01

The two promises

Plenty of tools will rewrite your resume with AI. These two things are what make this one different, and they are both structural, not marketing.

Moat one · privacy and cost

Bring your own AI key

You paste your own LLM API key (Claude, GPT, or another provider). Your CV, your job history, the posting, and every draft flow through your key, in your browser. We store none of it. There is no account holding your work history and no server to leak it from.

The same choice makes it essentially free to run: you pay your AI provider pennies per application, and we carry no inference cost that has to be recovered from you or your data.

Moat two · trust

Guardrails that will not embarrass you

  • ✓Pull, not invent. It tailors from a word bank of claims you wrote and approved. It rearranges and emphasizes your real experience. It does not create new experience.
  • ✓Every figure defensible. If a number or claim is not something you could stand behind in an interview, it does not go in.
  • ✓Truncated-posting check. If a job description is cut off (the LinkedIn "see more" trap), it flags it and refuses to tailor against a fraction of the requirements.
  • ✓You approve every word. Nothing is sent, saved, or finalized until you have read it and said yes.
  • ✓Your pattern, not a template. It learns how you actually open, address a gap honestly, and close, instead of pasting in the same opener every applicant uses.
02

How it works

One posting at a time, verified before anything is written, checked before anything is shown, approved before anything leaves.

Paste a job posting

Title plus the full description. That posting becomes the centre of everything that follows.

Gate Zero verifies it Guardrail

Is the posting real, live, and complete? Dead links, aggregator reposts, and truncated descriptions are caught here, before you spend a minute of effort on them.

It tailors, in your voice

The tool recommends a customization level (low, medium, or high), then tailors your CV summary and competencies and drafts a cover letter in your own pattern, using your own AI key.

Guardrails check the output Guardrail

Every draft is checked against your approved word bank: no fabricated claims, no invented figures, no words you have banned from your own writing.

You approve

You read the final CV and letter. You change what you want. Nothing ships without your yes.

Clean, formatted output

A polished CV and cover letter in a locked format that never drifts between applications.

Every posting is archived. Job postings vanish. If the role disappears the night before your interview, your copy of the description, and everything you prepared from it, is still there.

What refusing looks like

"This posting appears truncated: the description ends mid-sentence at 480 characters. Tailoring against a fraction of the requirements would misrepresent the fit. Paste the full description to continue."

03

Who this is for

The careful applicant

People who dread being caught out

If the idea of an AI-embellished claim surfacing in an interview makes your stomach drop, this tool was designed around exactly that fear. Everything it writes, you could defend at three in the morning.

The privacy-conscious

People who will not upload their history

Your CV is a map of your life. Bring-your-own-key means it stays on your machine, processed by a provider you chose, under terms you accepted. We are not in that loop.

The assisted search

A fractional job helper

The same engine can back a human-assisted service: a careful, honest operator running your search with you, using tools that cannot exaggerate on your behalf. One possible shape for this product.
